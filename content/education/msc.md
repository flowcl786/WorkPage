## Research

My thesis, *Integrating Statistical and Deep Learning Models for Time-Series Data Prediction*, combined statistical models (ARIMA, SMA) with LSTM to forecast across financial, environmental and public-health datasets. The work centred on building preprocessing pipelines, running forecasting experiments, and comparing model behaviour across datasets.

Full write-up: [Statistical + Deep Learning Models for Time-Series Prediction](/projects/time-series-thesis/).
